Cross-Site Synthesis

Turning an archive into a network, instead of just a longer list
What this is: An ordinary topic site answers "what is X." A synthesis essay answers "why X is the same thing across several of my sites — and where it stops being the same." When one structure keeps turning up in different fields, a result you worked hard for in one of them can simply be collected in another; this line goes looking for those channels. Every source is a page already published on this hub, and every section links straight back to it.
Syn 1 · Four fields, one number Entropy: The Same Number in Five Rooms Physics, information theory, machine learning and bioenergetics compute the same entropy — not a similar-looking formula, the same number up to a conversion constant. All four ask one question: given what I can see, how many possibilities remain underneath? Landauer's principle makes bits and joules convertible, and settled Maxwell's demon after seventy years. The fifth room, which borrowed only the shape of the word, comes last. 10 source pages · physics · mathematics · ai-ml · meta-knowledge · deep-reading · mental-models Read the essay → Syn 2 · One hand, three civilizations Evolutionary Games: One Hand, Three Civilizations Evolution dealt every human society the same hand — kin altruism, reciprocal altruism, tit-for-tat, group boundaries. What differs is which card each environment pushed to the front. The differential order, the spirit of contract, caste-and-renunciation are three stable equilibria natural selection sifted out on three different boards; and philosophy is the manual each writes for an operating system already running. Knowing it's the same hand is what lets you learn from how the others play it. 9 source pages · deep-reading · mental-models · psychology · philosophy · thinker-arena Read the essay →

Coming Up

Syn 3 · The Evolution of Cooperation — "Why does cooperation exist?" Four mutually incompatible answers: evolutionary biology does the accounting in genes, game theory in repeated play, international relations in credible commitment — and Buddhism refuses to do the accounting at all. The first three dissolve altruism into self-interest; the fourth overturns the table.
Syn 4 · The Self, and No-Self — Buddhism said "no-self" two thousand years ago; neuroscience arrives at nearly the same conclusion via attention schema theory. But don't let the convergence come cheap: one is a conclusion of practice, the other a value-neutral description of mechanism.
Syn 5 · Attention — The word travelled three times, from psychology to machine learning to economics, and the three mechanisms have nothing to do with each other: the QK dot product in a Transformer, selective gating in the thalamus, and attention traded as a scarce resource.
Syn 6 · Memory — Is "model memory" the same thing as human memory? An LLM's context window isn't memory, and neither is what sits in the weights — a confusion that costs real money in product design.
